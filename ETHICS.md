# Ethical and Responsible Use of AI

1. Data Privacy: No user data is stored. All inputs cleared after session.
2. Transparency: All AI-generated content is labeled.
3. Bias Mitigation: Prompts are neutral and inclusive.
4. Human-in-the-loop: AI assists, final decision by human.
5. Source Citation: Research module cites sources.
6. Compliance: No private or copyrighted data used.
